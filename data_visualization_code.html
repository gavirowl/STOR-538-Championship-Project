This heat map (Figure 1) was created to illustrate the count of corners played into each location on the field as well as the locations of players for each goal recorded. Each zone represents an area of the field recorded in our “Location” variable. Short passes were split based on whether they occurred on the right or left side of the field. In this figure, the left side of the field and front post are represented on the left half while the right side of the field and back post are represented on the right half. It’s important to note that we did not split front and back post by side of the field and the counts you see are the totals for front/back post, not counting the side of the field they were on. Each red X-mark represents the location of a goalscorer from where he kicked the ball into the goal. For example, one goal was scored from the corner kick itself thus, the X-mark is in the corner from which he scored.

This bar plot(Figure 2) was created to display the average amount of attackers in the box (A.in.Box) and in the 6-yard box (A.in.6) for each different outcome. Each outcome has two bars, the blue bar, representing the mean count of attackers in the box for each possible outcome, and the green bar, representing the mean count of attackers in the 6-yard box.

df_prop <- corners %>%
  mutate(LocationGroup = ifelse(Location == "Short", "Short", "Other")) %>%
  count(LocationGroup, Outcome) %>%
  group_by(LocationGroup) %>%
  mutate(prop = n / sum(n))

ggplot(df_prop, aes(x = LocationGroup, y = prop, fill = Outcome)) +
  geom_col() +
  labs(title = "Outcome Proportions: Short vs Other Corners (Figure 2)",
       x = "Corner Type",
       y = "Proportion",) +
  scale_fill_viridis_d(option = "turbo", name = "Outcome")+
  scale_y_continuous(labels = scales::percent) +
